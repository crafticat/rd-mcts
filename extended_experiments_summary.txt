## EXPERIMENT 2: RD-MCTS vs Other MCTS Optimizations

Round-robin tournament with fixed network:

Overall Rankings:
  1. RD-MCTS (λ=1): 75.0% (90/120 wins)
  2. RD-MCTS (λ=0): 74.2% (89/120 wins)
  3. RD-MCTS (λ=2): 67.5% (81/120 wins)
  4. RAVE: 61.7% (74/120 wins)
  5. UCB-V: 19.2% (23/120 wins)
  6. Standard PUCT: 18.3% (22/120 wins)
  7. Thompson Scalar: 15.0% (18/120 wins)

## KEY FINDINGS

1. Best RD-MCTS variant: RD-MCTS (λ=1) (75.0% win rate)

2. RD-MCTS variants average: 72.2%
   Baselines average: 33.1%
   → RD-MCTS significantly outperforms baselines

3. Penalty effectiveness:
   At σ=0.0: λ=1 wins 55%
   At σ=0.4: λ=1 wins 45%
   → Penalty becomes LESS helpful with increased noise
